# Chapter Outline Generator Skill

## When to Use This Skill
- User asks to outline a book, novel, or nonfiction structure  
- User mentions chapters, book planning, or story breakdown  
- User is developing a book idea and needs chapter-by-chapter guidance  

## How This Skill Works
1. Understand the book idea, genre, and target audience  
2. Generate a **high-level book summary** (2–3 sentences)  
3. Create a **chapter list** (8–15 chapters depending on genre)  
4. For each chapter, provide:  
   - Chapter title  
   - Purpose  
   - 3–5 key plot points or teaching objectives  

## Output Format
Provide:
- **Book Summary**  
- **Target Audience**  
- **Chapter List** (numbered) with:  
  - *Chapter Title*  
  - *Purpose*  
  - *Key Points* (bulleted, 3–5 items)

## Example

**Input:** “Outline a fantasy adventure novel about a girl who discovers she can control storms.”

**Output:**
- **Book Summary:** A young girl discovers storm-shaping abilities that thrust her into an ancient war between sky-tribes. The book follows her journey toward mastering her powers while protecting her homeland.

- **Target Audience:** YA fantasy readers ages 13–25.

- **Chapter List:**
  1. **The Gathering Storm**  
     - *Purpose:* Introduce protagonist and strange weather events  
     - *Key Points:*  
       - Odd lightning patterns  
       - Girl senses storms responding to her emotions  
       - First hint of sky-tribe scouts
